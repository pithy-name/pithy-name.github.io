About

Fixing the systems beneath the symptoms

Jane of All Trades

I spent over a decade building and running the quality function at a fast-moving startup in a constructively chaotic product environment where the ground shifted weekly. Instead of waiting for stability, I built flexible frameworks that scaled to the moment and a team of generalists who could take on anything.

The bigger bet I'm making now: AI Safety is fundamentally a trust problem; trust is fundamentally an understanding problem; and understanding is what mechanistic interpretability research is building — which is why I'm eager to dedicate the next stage of my career to supporting researchers and enabling them to do what only they can do.

Experience

Some things I've built and shaped

  • Developing people, not just performance

    Spearheaded the engineering career-leveling framework. Coached engineers from uncertainty to confidence, full-cycle: technical development, calibrated feedback, performance tied to a ladder I helped write.

  • Building technical functions from scratch

    Stood up a novel, cross-functional technical function with no template or playbook (except the ones I wrote). Defined what it should own, how it should sit across Engineering, Product, and Support, and how to measure it.

  • Founding culture and inclusion from zero

    Founded and ran the company's first employee resource group — a women's, cross-functional diversity-and-inclusion organization — with no line authority. Constituted a volunteer board and a full policy agenda in a single founding session, then grew it into a company-wide vehicle for culture and pay-equity influence.

Computational photography · 2026

Glamour Ships

Container ships slip past the Golden Gate all day. But often you catch only slices between buildings. This local-first pipeline turns slices into full bow-to-stern portraits: it listens to live AIS radio to predict a qualifying ship, triggers a mounted camera the moment it crosses the viewport, and slit-scans a thin strip from every frame into one seamless image no single frame ever saw whole.
