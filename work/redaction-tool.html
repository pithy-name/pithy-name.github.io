Deterministic Redaction Tooling

Pseudonymization for a decade of personal working notes.

Repository: github.com/pithy-name/local-tools

The problem

I wanted AI leverage over eleven years of professional notes — 1:1s, reviews, hiring records, incident retros. Those notes are full of other people. Names, initials, email handles, workplace specifics: none of it belongs anywhere near a third-party model.

The tempting shortcut is to ask an LLM to do the scrubbing. That's flawed: you'd be uploading the sensitive corpus to protect the sensitive corpus. The redaction layer has to sit below the AI line, else the line is fiction (The Emperor's New Clothes).
