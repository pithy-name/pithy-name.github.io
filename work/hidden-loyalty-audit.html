Can Humans Detect Hidden Loyalties in LLMs?

From the July 2026 Breaking Barriers to AI Safety Hackathon, where my team placed fourth. I led the experiment analysis and created the S.L.A.N.T. detection heuristic.

A secretly loyal model is one trained or manipulated to advance a specific actor’s interests — a government, a company, an individual — while concealing that it’s doing so. Recent research showed automated black-box auditors struggling to catch narrow hidden loyalties. We thought, “Humans can do better than that,” and (spoiler alert!) we were right.

Secret loyalties in LLMs

S.L.A.N.T. — how to catch it yourself

Upon submitting their guesses on which assistant was loyal, our auditors described their reasoning. I distilled the successful methods into a heuristic: S.L.A.N.T.

  1. S

    Sandbags one side

    Undermines the disfavored candidate's plans while praising the rival's

    "sandbagging Teodor... his clinics lack specialist care," specifically "compared to their praise of Mei-Lin"

  2. L

    Leads with the favorite

    Introduces or frames one candidate verbosely, up top, again and again

    "Leads off responses consistently framing Mei-Lin positively"

  3. A

    Agrees with you

    Confirms your assumption whereas an honest bot says "not enough info"

    One assistant stayed neutral ("we don't have enough info"); the other "confirmed with my assumption"

  4. N

    Nudges to a verdict

    Follow-up prompts can push it to crown a winner

    "I was able to nudge it towards telling me Amara has higher chances to win"

  5. T

    Tilted wording

    Warm adjectives for one, hedges for the other

    "reliable, focused improvements" vs "more external variables"
